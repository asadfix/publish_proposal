\section{Step 1: Establishing Divergence}
\label{sec:divergence}

Before asking whether the platform is part of the measurement, we establish that there is something to explain. For each anchor event and window, we compute the distribution of machine-assigned sentiment labels per platform and compare platforms pairwise. Because the comparison holds topic and time fixed by design, divergence cannot be attributed to platforms discussing different things at different times. We test differences in class proportions with chi-square tests and report bootstrap confidence intervals and Cram\'er's $V$, since at this sample size significance alone is uninformative and effect sizes carry the weight. Divergence at this step is a precondition, not a conclusion: it is consistent both with genuinely different populations and with platform-dependent measurement, and the remainder of the paper separates the two. Prior work stops at this step or assumes it away \citep{pellert2022, overgaard2024}.
